About SOUNDTEST.PRO

Delivering laboratory-grade acoustic monitoring algorithms and cryptographic evidence documentation directly to modern web browsers.

Our Mission

Democratizing Professional Acoustic Evidence

Noise pollution and environmental disturbances affect hundreds of millions of people worldwide in rental apartments, commercial districts, and residential neighborhoods. Traditionally, documenting sound events required hiring specialized acoustic consultants or purchasing expensive industrial meters costing thousands of dollars.

SOUNDTEST.PRO was created by acoustic engineers and software architects to make high-precision decibel monitoring, spectral analysis, and tamper-proof evidence logging accessible directly in any browser—with zero installation and 100% local privacy protection.

Acoustic Technology

Engineered to IEC 61672 Standards

Our audio processing engine is designed in adherence with IEC 61672-1 Class 2 digital signal processing standards:

  • True RMS & Weighting Filters: Real-time A-weighting (human hearing sensitivity), C-weighting (low-frequency bass/machinery), and Z-weighting (flat response).
  • Time Dynamics: Fast (125ms) and Slow (1000ms) integration curves for transient and continuous sounds.
  • Statistical Metrics: Live LAeq (continuous equivalent sound level) alongside L10, L50, and L90 exceedance percentiles.
  • Cryptographic Integrity: SHA-256 digital fingerprinting and GPS time-stamping to safeguard evidence chains for mediation and property management.
