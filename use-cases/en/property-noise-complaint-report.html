Practical Advice

Four golden rules for impartial property investigations

Property managers must maintain neutrality between feuding neighbors. Here is how to keep your reports beyond reproach.

⚖️
Rule 1: Always verify inside the complainant's home first Stand where the resident claims to hear the noise. If the level inside their bedroom does not exceed 35 dBA, the complaint may stem from heightened sensitivity.
🚪
Rule 2: Measure at the corridor threshold of the alleged source Take a second measurement 1 meter outside the source unit's door. If noise exceeds 65 dBA in the hallway, bylaw sound transmission covenants are clearly violated.
📄
Rule 3: Provide identical PDF summaries to both parties Sharing the objective SOUNDTEST.PRO PDF dossier removes personal bias accusations and encourages mutual compromise before formal fines are levied.
⚖️
Rule 4: Understand the civilian evidence boundary SOUNDTEST.PRO provides internal management documentation. If escalated to court litigation, supplement with certified Class 1 acoustic engineer reports.
