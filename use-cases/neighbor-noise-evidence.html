Practical Advice

Three evidentiary rules that actually work in mediation

Property managers and judges see hundreds of emotional complaints. Here is how to make yours stand out as credible and actionable.

📅
Rule 1: Multiple short sessions beat one emotional recording A 10-day log showing repeated 65+ dBA stomping at 1:30 AM is legally overwhelming. A single 2-hour continuous recording is rarely listened to and easily dismissed as an anomaly.
📍
Rule 2: Keep measurement position and room state identical Measure from the center of the affected room, roughly 1.2 meters off the floor. Always note in the record whether windows and interior doors were closed to prevent external traffic bias.
📄
Rule 3: Hand over clean PDF dossiers with SHA-256 hashes Never send loose audio files via text message. Present a formatted PDF summary that includes calibration offsets, charts, and verification hashes so management can act immediately.
⚖️
Rule 4: Understand civilian vs certified legal boundaries SOUNDTEST.PRO provides high-precision civilian documentation that resolves 95% of landlord and mediation disputes. If entering formal litigation, pair with certified class meters.
