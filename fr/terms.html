2. Description of the Service

SOUNDTEST.PRO provides an advanced browser-native acoustic measurement and digital evidence documentation platform. Features include real-time decibel estimation (dBA, dBC, dBZ), equivalent continuous sound levels (LAeq), statistical sound level percentiles (L10, L50, L90), Fast/Slow time weightings, FFT frequency spectrum analysis, cryptographic SHA-256 evidence hashing, GPS tagging, and exportable PDF/CSV reports.

Our signal processing algorithms are engineered in accordance with IEC 61672-1 Class 2 acoustic measurement principles. All microphone analysis and media recordings remain local to your browser and device.
