Quatre capacités clés

Mesurer, enregistrer, horodater et exporter — un flux serein.

Built for people who arrive anxious about noise and need a calm, professional way to lock the facts before they speak with a landlord, manager, or adviser.

Sonomètre en temps réel

Accurate sound level detection: live dB / dBA, peak, average, LAeq, and L5/L10/L50/L90/L95 percentile summaries — all computed locally on your device.

Enregistrement audio et forme d'onde

Record noise with waveform and spectrum context to keep complete, local sound documentation. Waveform, spectrum chart, and timestamps stay with the file.

Horodatage et géolocalisation automatiques

Automatically record date, time, and geographic location when permission is granted — the metadata you need to make a complaint hold up under scrutiny.

Export du rapport de preuve

Generate structured PDF, CSV, and JSON reports — official-feeling templates ready for property management, local authority, or legal advisers.
